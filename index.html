About

How we think

Three operating principles that shape everything we research and build.

01
Research First
Before building original models, we study the canonical ones deeply — the frameworks, structures, and problem-solving methods developed by the world's leading strategy firms. Understanding what exists is the foundation for what comes next.
02
Learn in Public
We publish our thinking as it develops — not when it's finished, but when it's useful. The process is the product.
03
Build to Understand
The best way to test whether you've understood a framework is to build something from it. The Lab exists because of this belief.

USR started as a question: why do the best consulting firms think so differently from everyone else? Not just their outputs — their process. The way they break a problem apart, structure uncertainty, and move from ambiguous data to a clear recommendation.

The answer isn't a secret framework. It's a set of thinking habits that can be learned, practiced, and eventually extended. That's what we're building toward.

"Most strategy advice tells you what to think. We're more interested in how to think."
— USR, on the approach
