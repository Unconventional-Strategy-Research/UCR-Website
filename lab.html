What it does
Active recall over passive reading
CaseIQ is built on a single premise: you don't learn consulting frameworks by reading them — you learn them by applying them under pressure, repeatedly, until the reasoning becomes automatic.

Every module pairs a short reference sheet with an immediate drill. The questions aren't recall tests — they're application problems that force you to choose between plausible but meaningfully different answers, the same kind of judgment calls that surface in real interviews.
Why it tracks stats
The pattern you can't see yourself
A single run tells you your score. Repeated runs reveal something more useful: which cognitive move consistently breaks down.

Most candidates who struggle with cases aren't weak across the board — they have a specific failure mode. They can recognise a framework but can't apply it. They can apply a framework but can't synthesise a recommendation. CaseIQ's stats are designed to surface that failure mode precisely, so you can fix the right thing.

Lab Philosophy

Why we build

Every tool in the Lab starts the same way: we encounter a framework we want to understand better, and we ask what would it look like to make this trainable?

CaseIQ exists because turning six consulting frameworks into 76 questions forced us to understand them at a level that reading alone doesn't produce. You can't write a good question about hypothesis generation without knowing exactly where candidates go wrong.

The Lab is the research made tangible. It's also how we test whether our synthesis is actually right — if we can't build a working tool from a framework, we probably don't understand it well enough yet.

Everything here is free. No accounts, no paywalls. The community that uses these tools is the same community that shapes what gets built next.
